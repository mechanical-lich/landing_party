# Factions

You are not alone out here. Other factions grow on the worlds of the Star
Map, spread from world to world while you are elsewhere, and land on the
worlds you have settled. This page covers what they are, how you see them,
and the New Expedition options that decide who you share the map with.

Each faction has a page of its own:

| Faction | Page | Spreads by | Toward you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Xenos and troids | [Xenos and Troids](xenos_and_troids.md) | eggs fired from world to world | hostile |
| Autonz | [Autonz](autonz.md) | signals that wake sleeping machines | hostile |
| Greys | [Greys](greys.md) | saucers and a mothership | hostile |
| Rival colonies | [Rival Colonies](rival_colonies.md) | a ship like yours | neutral until you land on their world |

Three of them can also be played: see [Playing another race](#playing-another-race).

---

## How factions live

A faction holds worlds. On each world it holds it has a **presence**: a
nest, a machine node, a grey camp or a rival colony. A presence grows
toward what its world can support, researches, and, when it is big enough,
spreads to another world. The xenos fire eggs, the autonz send signals,
and the greys and rival colonies fly ships.

While you are not on a world, its factions grow there day by day. When you
land, everything they have is built onto the map and runs like your own
colony does: their workers mine, haul, build, research and fight, using
real materials from real storage. When you leave, the world is paused
again and its factions go back to growing day by day.

Factions never spread onto the first worlds near the Wreck Site until you
have visited them, so your first landing is always clean.

## Seeing them

You only see what your expedition has found. A presence is unseen until:

- your ship is in **orbit** of its world (the game posts an alert such as
  "Xenos presence detected on Rust Tangle."),
- a **ship scan** sweeps its world (see [Scanning](star_map.md#scanning-for-new-worlds)), or
- for eggs and ships in flight, they come within your scanner's range of
  where your ship is.

### On the Star Map

Each faction has a colour. A world it holds gets a **badge** beside its icon:

| Badge | Faction |
|---|---|
| Diamond, one pip per stage | xenos and troids |
| Square chip, one pip per stage; outlined while asleep | autonz |
| Saucer (a larger saucer with a ring is a mothership) | greys |
| Dart | rival colony |

The world's info panel adds a **tag** under its Calm / Hazard / Threat tag,
for example `XENO NEST`, `AUTONZ HUB`, `GREY SCOUT · HOSTILE` or
`RIVAL COLONY · NEUTRAL`. Things heading for a world show there too:
`XENO EGG INBOUND · 12 DAYS`, `GREY FREIGHTER INBOUND · 3 DAYS`,
`AUTONZ SIGNAL · WAKES IN 20 DAYS`. A ship that has left says where it went:
`GREY SCOUT BOUND FOR EMBER PIT · 4 DAYS`. The Destinations list notes the
faction in brackets after the world's name: `[xeno nest]`,
`[autonz hub]`, `[grey mothership]`, `[rival colony]`.

Eggs and ships in flight are drawn at their position with a dashed line to
where they are going.

When there are two factions of the same kind, the second is numbered:
"Xenos 2".

### The Factions tab

The **Factions** tab of the dashboard (**O** or the Star Map button, beside
Legends) shows every faction you have seen. Each gets a block in its colour
with its total population, its research and its counts, then a row for
each world it holds (stage, population, how much of the ground it has
converted; an asleep autonz signal with its wake day) and for each ship or
egg in flight (its class, crew and cargo, where it is or where it is going,
and when it arrives). Click a world row to select that world on the Star
Map; click a ship row to select the world it is at or bound for. The tab
updates once a star-date day.

Xenos and troids appear as separate blocks, named by what you have seen.

### Legends

What factions do is written into the [Legends](legends.md) chronicle:
arrivals, nests growing, eggs fired, ships landing and lifting off, signals
sent and nodes waking, a faction turning hostile, ships boarded and taken,
presences wiped out. Things that happened where you could not see are
recorded too, and appear once you find the faction they belong to.

## Hostile and neutral

Most factions are **hostile** from the start. A hostile faction's workers
are things your colonists will fight, and its fighters will come for you:

- Hostile factions **raid** only once they have found you. Each one has
  its own way of finding you (xeno scouts, autonz seekers, grey patrols, or
  simply the rival colony seeing you land), and a raid is sent only when
  your colonists are on its world. An alert names the raiders.
- A raid goes for your colonist nearest its camp, or failing that a storage
  container of yours.

A **neutral** faction leaves you alone and your colonists leave it alone.
The rival colony starts neutral, and turns hostile everywhere, at once, the
first time you beam a colonist down onto a world it holds. See
[Rival Colonies](rival_colonies.md).

### Groups

Every faction, yours included, is in a **group**. You start in group 1.

- **Same group: friendly.** A faction in your group is neutral to you and
  never turns hostile, whatever you do. Its people and yours do not fight.
  It never spreads onto a world your side already holds.
- **Different groups: hostile.** Factions in different groups fight when
  their people meet on a world. A rival colony in another group is still
  neutral to you until you land on its world.

Factions only fight each other on a world you are playing. Between worlds,
two factions do not attack each other.

## Meeting ships in space

Grey ships and rival colony ships fly between worlds, and your jumps can
cross them. A jump **meets** a ship when its line passes close to a ship in
flight, or when you arrive at a world a grey mothership (or a ship docked
at it) is orbiting. Eggs are never met, and ships on the ground are not in
space.

The clock stops and a **Contact** popup says what you met ("A grey scout
crosses your path on the way to Ember Pit."). Answer it:

| Answer | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Evade** | Costs fuel from the hold. It can fail; then you are boarded anyway. |
| **Board** | The ship's deck becomes a place on the Star Map and your ship goes there for free. |
| **Parley** | Only offered when the faction is neutral to you. Both ships go on. |

| Ship | Evade fuel | Chance to be caught |
|---|---|---|
| Rival colony ship, grey scout | 2 | 10% |
| Grey freighter | 3 | 25% |
| Grey warship | 5 | 45% |
| Grey mothership | 8 | 70% |

Grey ships that have researched **Tractor Lock** catch you 10% more often,
and those with **Cloak** have to pass closer to your line to meet you.

**Boarding.** Beam a party onto the deck. The crew are aboard in their
fighting forms and the ship's hold is in a locker. Kill the whole crew and
the ship is yours: the faction loses it, and the hold is your loot. Leave
the deck with any of the crew still alive and the ship flies on with them.
For boarding the grey mothership see [Greys](greys.md#the-mothership).

Every answer is decided from the campaign seed, so loading a save and
answering the same way gives the same result.

## New Expedition options

The New Expedition screen has three faction settings.

### Race

The **Race** row picks who you play. Use **<** and **>** to step through.
A short description of the race sits under its name.

| Race | Run | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Colony (the default, listed first) | The ship and the Star Map, as the rest of this guide describes | [Getting Started](README.md#getting-started) |
| Hive | No ship. Spread a brood across six worlds. | [Playing the hive](xenos_and_troids.md#playing-the-hive) (a spoiler for the xenos and troids) |
| Autonz | No ship. Wire six worlds and signal between them. | [Autonz: playing the autonz](autonz.md#playing-the-autonz) |
| Greyish | A saucer that grows, a grey crew, the colony's open-ended run. | [Greys: playing the greys](greys.md#playing-the-greys) |

The guided first landing is only for the colony; the tutorial toggle is
hidden for the other races.

### Rival factions

**Rival factions** is how many factions besides you are on the map at the
start: 0 to 4, default 1. With 0 nothing else grows anywhere.

Under it is one row for each rival. Each row has:

- **The faction**, chosen with **<** and **>**: Random, Autonz, Greyish,
  Hive or Rival Colony. Random rolls one from
  the expedition's seed, and does
  not repeat a faction until each has come up once. You can pick the same
  faction twice; the second is its own faction with its own worlds.
- **grp**, its group, set with **-** and **+** (1 to 9). By default every
  rival has a group of its own (rival 1 in group 2, rival 2 in group 3, and
  so on), so they are hostile to you and to each other.

**Your group** sets your own group (1 to 9, default 1). Put a rival in the
same group as you to have it as a friend; put two rivals in the same group
to have them side with each other.

More rival factions spread faster: each one shortens the time between the
xenos' egg shots and the autonz' signals.

Each rival arrives from outside the galaxy at its own point on the rim, never yours. A grey rival
also starts with a mothership in orbit of the world its first ship landed on.

A **rival colony** wants to leave the galaxy. Its ship heads deep into the
galaxy until it finds the gate, flies to the world holding the gate's
controls, switches the gate on there (you may meet it in the vault) and goes
through. You hear when it switches the gate on, and when it leaves if you
had met it. See [The gate](star_map.md#the-gate).

## Playing another race

The hive and the autonz have **no ship**. You hold worlds instead of
flying between them; holding six at once is a milestone.

- **View** on the Star Map opens one of your worlds, or a world within
  your reach, as a ship in orbit would see it. On a world you hold you can
  give orders; on one you only reach, you can look.
- **The director helps.** On each of your worlds a director runs your
  people the way a rival's camp is run, and your orders go into the same
  queue. **Take Over** in the Orders menu opens the **This World** panel on
  any world you hold, where you take over the whole world or parts of the
  work (see each race's page). Leaving a world hands everything you took
  over back to the director.
- **You spread by hand.** Your Resin Spire or Signal Core only fires at a
  world you have aimed it at. Unaimed, it waits.
- The Star Map tags your worlds `[yours]` and lists them under **Your
  worlds** with their stage and population, and anything of yours in
  flight. The dashboard header shows `Your worlds: 3 / 6`.
- A world at its launching stage identifies every world within its reach
  once a day ("New world in reach: Ember Pit").
- There are no quests, no incidents, no beaming and no tutorial.
- Holding six worlds at once is a **milestone** (an alert and a Legends
  entry; the run goes on). You **lose** when nothing of yours is left
  anywhere: no world and nothing in flight.
- The rival colony is hostile to you once its ship lands on a world you
  hold, or once one of your people comes within 8 tiles of one of its
  colonists. An alert says so: "Your brood have been seen on Ember Pit.
  They are hostile now." ("Your units" for the autonz).

The greys fly a ship like the colony does: the same Star Map, quests,
beaming and open-ended run, with a grey crew and a saucer that grows. See
[Greys: playing the greys](greys.md#playing-the-greys).
