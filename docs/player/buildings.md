# Buildings

Structures are placed via the build menu and constructed by colonists. Most require materials and some require research to unlock.

---

## Structure Reference

### Hull Wall

Solid wall tile. Blocks movement and line of sight. Use to define rooms and perimeter defenses.

### Hull Floor

Passable floor tile. Required as a foundation for interior structures in most layouts.

### Storage Locker

Container for items and resources. Colonists haul gathered materials to nearby storage lockers and retrieve food from them when hungry. Place one in every work area.

### Research Lab

Allows colonists to perform research tasks. A colonist assigned to research must be adjacent to (or inside) a research lab for the task to progress. Multiple labs allow multiple colonists to research simultaneously — progress accumulates faster.

See [Research](research.md) for the full tech tree.

### Workbench

Enables crafting tasks. Colonists must be adjacent to a workbench to craft items. See the crafting recipes for what can be produced.

### Work Light

Provides illumination at night. Place these throughout your colony to maintain visibility and worker productivity during the dark cycle.

### Airlock

A two-stage door that cycles air between sections. The airlock system recognizes faction membership — your colonists pass through freely; hostile entities are blocked. Useful for maintaining a secure perimeter with controlled access.

### Blast Door

Heavy reinforced door. Significantly more durable than a standard airlock. Use on outer perimeter entrances where hostile pressure is highest.

### Stairs

Connects adjacent Z-levels. Required for colonists to move between underground, surface, and upper levels.

---

## Construction

When you place a structure:

1. The build site appears on the map as a blueprint overlay.
2. Colonists with available capacity claim a haul task to deliver required materials.
3. Once materials arrive, a colonist claims the construction task and works until the structure is complete.
4. Progress on construction is shared — multiple colonists speed up completion.

Structures with zero material cost (currently all structures during the testing phase) complete as soon as a colonist begins working on them.
