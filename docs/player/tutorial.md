# The Guided First Landing

A new expedition can start with a short guided tutorial. It is on by default on
the **New Expedition** screen ("Guided first landing"); turn the toggle off and
the game generates and plays exactly as it does without it. The galaxy is the
same for a given seed either way, apart from one extra planet.

## What it does

You start the normal way: adrift at the Wreck Site, crew aboard, looking at the
Star Map with nothing to land on. The tutorial adds one small, safe planet
nearer than anything else, the **Training Ground**, and walks you through 24
short steps in four phases:

1. **Choose.** The Training Ground is marked in magenta on the Star Map and in
   the destination list, already selected and centred, with its fuel cost
   showing. Press Travel Here. Every other destination is locked until the
   first landing is done: its button reads "Travel locked" and the status line
   says where to go instead.
2. **Land.** Beam two colonists down, learn the camera, depth levels, pausing,
   selecting a colonist, hauling the rations lying on the ground, beaming the
   metal down from the ship's supply pad, building a locker, digging, mining,
   storage, needs, crafting and research.
3. **Pack up.** Beam your mined ore and crystal up to the ship, beam the crew
   up, and jump to the system nearest the wreck, which is the one new
   destination.
4. **Build.** Land there, beam the metal down and build a storage locker from
   it. The tutorial ends, the lock lifts everywhere, and the random campaign
   carries on as it is.

A tutorial expedition starts with only **10 metal** in the hold (everything else
is normal). That is what you beam down on the Training Ground, so the locker on
the second planet can only be built from ore you mined and packed yourself.

## The prompt panel

Prompts never pause the game. The panel sits above the resource bar on a planet
and top right on the dashboard. Each prompt names the step, says what to do and
highlights the button, list, planet or spot it is talking about. It moves on
when you have done the thing, so doing things early or in another order just
skips those prompts. If you are in the wrong place for a step the panel tells
you where to go.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **–** | Collapses the panel to its title row, so nothing is ever stuck under it |
| **Manual** | Opens the step's article in the Encyclopedia's Field Manual |
| **Skip tutorial** | Two clicks ("Really skip?"). Ends the tutorial where you are and lifts the Travel lock; the world is left exactly as it is |

When the tutorial finishes or you skip it, the final panel has a toggle,
**Offer the tutorial on new expeditions**. Turn it off and the New Expedition
toggle defaults to off from then on; you can still flip it each time.

## Things worth knowing

- **Food.** The Training Ground has ration packs on the ground. Colonists do not
  pick up loose items on their own; click a ration (or use Store) to have it
  carried to the supply pad. Crew aboard the ship eat from the hold's rations.
- **Mining takes hauling.** Ore and crystal are mined quickly and dropped beside
  the miner one unit at a time, then carried to storage one unit per trip. A
  whole deposit means many trips, so give the mine order and get on with the
  other steps.
- **The supply pad.** The Training Ground starts with a colony-owned pad that
  accepts everything. Beam Resources Down needs a container on the site; if the
  second planet has none, one is set down for you on arrival.
- **The second planet is not safe.** It is your real start system, hostiles and
  all. Land, beam the metal down, build the locker and decide what to do next
  with the crew on the ship.
- **Saving.** Progress is saved with the expedition. A loaded game resumes at
  the step it was on, with the Travel lock as it was.

The Field Manual stays in the Encyclopedia with the tutorial on, off, finished
or skipped. See [Encyclopedia](encyclopedia.md).
