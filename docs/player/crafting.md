# Crafting

Crafting lets you turn raw resources and salvaged materials into weapons, armor, and consumables. All crafting happens at a **workbench**.

---

## Workbenches

Each workbench is specialized. You will need to build the right one for what you want to make:

| Bench | Makes |
|-------|-------|
| **Workbench** | General-purpose items like medkits |
| **Gun Bench** | Ranged weapons — pistols, rifles, shotguns, plasma cutters |
| **Armor Bench** | Light armor — enviro suits |
| **Advanced Armor Bench** | Heavy armor (requires Advanced Metallurgy research) |

Place a bench in a secure room, ideally near a storage locker so colonists can pick up materials quickly.

---

## Queueing a Recipe

1. Click a placed workbench to open its crafting menu.
2. Select a recipe. Greyed-out recipes are missing materials or a required tech.
3. The recipe is added to the settlement task queue.
4. A colonist with sufficient Intelligence claims the task, hauls the required materials from storage, and works at the bench until the item is finished.
5. Completed items appear in the bench's inventory — assign a colonist to pick them up, or have one haul the item to a storage locker.

Multiple colonists can speed up production by running parallel crafting tasks at multiple benches of the same type.

---

## Materials

Recipes consume resources gathered or salvaged from the world:

- **Metal ore** — mined from ore deposits
- **Crystal** — mined from crystal veins
- **Stone** — mined from rock
- **Biomass** — gathered from alien flora and critters

Some recipes are currently free (testing phase). Others, like medkits, require biomass.

---

## Tips

- Build a Gun Bench early. Even one rifle dramatically improves a colonist's ability to fend off aliens that breach the perimeter.
- Medkits are cheap and save lives — keep a few in storage at all times.
- The Advanced Armor Bench is gated behind Advanced Metallurgy research. Plan your tech path if you want heavy armor before nighttime hostiles ramp up.
