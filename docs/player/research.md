# Research

Research unlocks new buildings and capabilities. Assign colonists to a research task near a **research lab** to advance it.

---

## How Research Works

1. Open the research menu and select a technology to study.
2. A research task is added to the settlement's task queue.
3. Colonists with access to a research lab will claim and work the task.
4. Multiple colonists can work the same research task simultaneously — progress is cumulative.
5. When complete, the technology is added to your settlement's known techs and any buildings or recipes it unlocks become available.

Some technologies require a **prerequisite** to be researched first.

---

## Tech Tree

### Basic Metallurgy

Foundational materials knowledge. No prerequisites.

**Unlocks:** Advanced structural builds, metal-based construction recipes.

---

### Advanced Metallurgy

Refined alloy techniques for heavier construction.

**Requires:** Basic Metallurgy

---

### Xenobiology

Study of alien biology and ecosystems. Useful for understanding and countering the hostile creatures on the planet surface.

**Requires:** None

---

### Power Systems

Electrical generation and distribution. Required for powered structures and lighting upgrades.

**Requires:** None

---

### Inventory Survey

Networked locker telemetry for a single site. Unlocks the **Current Site** tab in the Global Inventory modal (Star Map → Global Inventory).

**Requires:** None

---

### Fleet Census

Subspace ledger that aggregates storage across the ship hold and every established site. Unlocks the **All Sites** tab in the Global Inventory.

**Requires:** Inventory Survey

---

### Detailed Census

Per-site breakdown of the fleet census. Unlocks the **By Site** tab in the Global Inventory — pick any established location (or the ship hold) and see its individual stockpile.

**Requires:** Fleet Census
