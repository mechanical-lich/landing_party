# Rival Colonies

A rival colony is another expedition like yours: colonists from a ship,
building the same structures, researching the same tree and eating the same
rations. It is run by another faction, not by you. It leaves you alone
until you land on its world.

See [Factions](factions.md) for how factions in general spread, show on the
Star Map and turn hostile.

---

## How a rival colony moves

A rival colony lives on its ship. It starts landed on a world with **4
colonists** and a kit of 30 metal ore, 15 crystal and 12 ration packs.

- While landed, it builds a camp, grows its crew toward what the world can
  hold, mines and researches.
- After at least 4 days it may move on, when another world in reach looks
  better than where it is. It always leaves after **40 days**, and it packs
  up early if it is down to its last colonist.
- It flies to a free world (no other faction there) within 40 of where it
  is, in the same units as the Star Map's fuel cost, at 4 a day. Its ship
  carries 80 fuel and refuels slowly while landed.
- Everyone goes with the ship, and so does everything in its storage.

## On the Star Map

The ship is drawn as a dart in the colony's colour, beside the world it
holds or in flight with a dashed line to where it is going.

| Where | Tag |
|---|---|
| landed on this world | `RIVAL COLONY · NEUTRAL` or `RIVAL COLONY · HOSTILE` |
| heading here | `RIVAL COLONY SHIP INBOUND · 3 DAYS` |
| left this world | `RIVAL COLONY SHIP BOUND FOR EMBER PIT · 4 DAYS` |

The Destinations list notes `[rival colony]`. Legends records it landing
("its crew made camp"), lifting off and turning hostile.

## On the ground

The camp is a bunker room well away from your landing, with an airlock on
its south side, a storage locker and a workbench. Its colonists look like
yours. They put up beds, lockers, a workbench, a research lab and a gun
bench, research the colony tree, craft stun batons, and hunt critters for
food when they get hungry. They never join your crew.

## Neutral, then hostile

A rival colony starts **neutral**: your colonists and its colonists leave
each other alone, and if you meet its ship in space you can **Parley** and
both go on.

The first time you beam a colonist down onto a world it holds, it takes
your landing as a threat and turns **hostile everywhere**, at once. An
alert and a Legends line record it. From then on:

- its colonists are targets for yours,
- it arms colonists with its stun batons and **raids** you with two or
  three of them, while you have colonists on its world,
- Parley is no longer offered.

A rival colony in your own group never turns hostile. See
[Groups](factions.md#groups).

## Fighting and looting

- **Kill all its colonists** on a world you are playing and the colony is
  wiped out: its ship is gone and its camp stays as it stood, with
  whatever is in its storage.
- **Meet its ship in space** and you can Evade (2 fuel, caught 10% of the
  time) or Board it. Its crew fights you on the deck and its hold is in a
  locker aboard. See [Meeting ships in space](factions.md#meeting-ships-in-space).
- **When it lifts off** it leaves its camp behind as a **ruin**: broken
  walls and floors, empty storage, and a datapad that records where it
  went. See [Dangers, Ruins and Relics](dangers_and_ruins.md#what-factions-leave-behind).

## Units, structures and research

A rival colony uses the colony's own content:

- **Colonists** as described in [Colonists](colonists.md).
- **Structures** from [Buildings](buildings.md).
- **Research** from the [Research](research.md) tree.
- **Recipes** from [Crafting](crafting.md).
