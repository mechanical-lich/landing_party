# Dangers, Ruins and Relics

Every world you chart has a past, and most have something waiting in it. This
page covers what the Star Map tells you before you land, the hazards you can
meet on the ground, and what old peoples left behind.

## Danger tags

A charted world's summary on the [Star Map](star_map.md) starts with a tag:

| Tag | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Calm** (blue-grey) | Nothing on the world is out to get you. Wildlife keeps its distance. |
| **Hazard** (amber) | Something local and finite: a leak, a nest, a few hostiles, a trap. Dangerous near it, quiet elsewhere. |
| **Threat** (red) | A full scenario: something on this world spreads, hunts or keeps coming. |

The tag tells you how bad, not what; the line after it hints at what is
going on. The first worlds near the Wreck Site are never threats.

The tag is the world's own story. Other [factions](factions.md) living there
get a tag of their own under it.

## Hazards you can meet

Hazards sit where they started, never right beside your landing.

| Hazard | How it behaves |
|---|---|
| Leaks and fallout (old reactors, wrecked drives, war) | Radiation around the spot; bring rad gear. |
| Crystal patch | Glowing crystal and a little radiation. |
| Worm sign, troid clutch, dormant nest | A few worms in the rock, or eggs waiting to hatch near an old wreck or crater. |
| Hostile fauna, mutant band, restless graves, survivors of an old war | A small group of hostiles around their spot. |
| Dormant machine | One enforcer powered down inside an old hull. |
| **Spore pods** | Pods that burst at any colonist standing next to one. They never move; cut them down or walk around. |
| **Collapsed ground** | A cave that fell in: rubble to dig through, and something buried under it. |
| **Old defences** | Sentry turrets behind a low wall. They sleep until someone comes within about 7 tiles, then fire for good. |
| **Unexploded shells** | Shells left on an old battlefield. One goes off if a colonist steps beside it or digs it up. |
| **Breached station section** | A part of a station open to space. Nobody gets in without a full enviro suit; what is stored inside waits for whoever does. |

## Ruins, ships and relics

The places old peoples built still stand where they built them: villages,
castles with their towns, forts, mines, graveyards, towers, bunkers, landing pads, and the
ships that came down, from scouts to warships. Older ones are more worn;
some are rubble.

- **Named rooms.** Hover over a room inside one to see what it was:
  "Bridge", "Cargo Hold", "Isolation Ward", "Barracks".
- **Datapads.** Each one says why it was there, read from the datapad left
  inside (a ship's log on its bridge, a ledger in an office). Picking it up
  adds the story to [Legends](legends.md).
- **Lockers.** What they kept is still in their lockers: cargo, provisions,
  weapons, grave goods.
- **Relics.** Every people leaves something of its age: carved idols and old
  blades from early peoples, machine parts and fission cores from industrial
  and atomic ones, tech fragments and alien alloy from those that crossed the
  stars.

## What factions leave behind

| What | Left by | What is there |
|---|---|---|
| **Husk** | a nest that died out on its own | an empty nest with cracked eggs ([Xenos and Troids](xenos_and_troids.md)) |
| **Ruined camp** | a rival colony that lifted off | its camp, broken, with empty storage and a datapad saying where it went ([Rival Colonies](rival_colonies.md)) |
| **Landing scar** | a grey ship that lifted off | scorched ground, empty unowned lockers, and wrecked armories, arrays and emitters to break for grey alloy and crystal ([Greys](greys.md)) |
| **Dormant Node** | an autonz signal | a single dark machine that wakes 30 days after it landed; destroy it first and the world stays clean ([Autonz](autonz.md)) |
| **Camp** | a rival colony or grey ship whose crew you killed | the camp stays as it stood |

A faction's datapad adds its line to [Legends](legends.md) when picked up.
