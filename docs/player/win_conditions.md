# Win Conditions

Each scenario defines its own win and loss conditions. See [Scenarios](scenarios.md) for the full list of game modes; the most common shape is "survive N days without losing all colonists."

---

## Survival Scenario

### Win

**Survive for 30 days.**

The day counter is visible in the HUD. Reach day 30 with at least one colonist alive and the scenario ends in victory.

### Lose

**All colonists are dead.**

If every colonist in your settlement dies — from starvation, hostile attack, or any other cause — the scenario ends immediately in defeat.

---

## Tips

- Prioritize food supply early. Starving colonists are the most common cause of early loss.
- Build blast doors on your perimeter before hostiles begin arriving in force.
- Research labs multiply your research speed — build several.
- Hostile spawn rates increase over time. The first few days are relatively calm; plan your defenses before the pressure builds.
